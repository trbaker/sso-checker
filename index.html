Enterprise login check for ArcGIS Online

Paste one or more organization URL prefixes — the myorg in myorg.maps.arcgis.com — and find out which ones have SAML or OpenID Connect logins turned on. Nothing here requires signing in; each organization is asked for its public sign-in options.

Full URLs are fine too; the prefix will be pulled out of them.
