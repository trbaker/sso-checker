Enterprise login check for ArcGIS Online

Version 1.0

Paste one or more organization URL prefixes — the myorg in myorg.maps.arcgis.com — and find out which ones have SAML or OpenID Connect logins turned on. Nothing here requires signing in; each organization's public sign-in page is read (by check.php on tbaker.com) and checked for an enterprise identity provider.

Full URLs are fine too; the prefix will be pulled out of them.
